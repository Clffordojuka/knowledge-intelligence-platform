LOAN OPERATIONS STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

1. Application Registration
Every loan application must be assigned a unique application reference.
Operations personnel must record the applicant's details, submission date,
requested loan amount, and application status.

2. Document Verification
Operations personnel must confirm that all required application documents
have been received. Identity documents must be checked for completeness and
consistency with the information submitted by the applicant.

3. Application Processing
Applications with missing documentation must be returned for clarification.
Applications with complete documentation may proceed to credit assessment.
The application status must be updated at every major processing stage.

4. Loan Disbursement
Approved loan applications may proceed to disbursement after all required
approval conditions have been satisfied. Disbursement records must include
the approved amount, transaction reference, and disbursement date.

5. Record Retention
Application records and supporting documents must be retained in accordance
with the institution's records-management requirements.
