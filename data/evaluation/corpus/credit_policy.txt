CREDIT POLICY

1. Borrower Eligibility
All loan applicants must provide valid government-issued photo identification.
Applicants must meet the institution's minimum eligibility requirements before
a credit application can proceed.

2. Repayment Capacity Assessment
Credit officers must assess monthly disposable income after existing debt
obligations. The maximum permitted debt-service ratio is 40 percent.
Applicants exceeding this threshold must not receive standard loan approval
without an authorized exception.

3. Credit History Review
Credit officers must review the applicant's credit history, including
unresolved defaults and delinquent repayment accounts. Adverse credit
information must be considered when determining borrower risk.

4. Credit Approval
Applications meeting the eligibility, repayment capacity, and credit-history
requirements may proceed through the standard approval workflow. Exceptions
must follow the institution's designated escalation and approval procedures.
