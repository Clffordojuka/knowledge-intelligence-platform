COMPLIANCE AND RECORDKEEPING POLICY

1. Customer Due Diligence
The institution must perform customer due diligence before establishing
a lending relationship. Customer identity and relevant customer information
must be verified in accordance with applicable compliance requirements.

2. Anti-Money Laundering Controls
Customer and transaction activity must be reviewed for indicators of
potential money laundering or other suspicious activity. Identified concerns
must be handled through the institution's established reporting procedures.

3. Auditability
Credit application records must retain identity verification findings,
repayment capacity calculations, and credit-history review outcomes.
Records must provide sufficient evidence to reconstruct the decisions made
during the credit assessment process.

4. Access and Confidentiality
Customer information must only be accessed by personnel with an authorized
business need. Customer records must be protected against unauthorized
disclosure, alteration, or loss.

5. Compliance Monitoring
Compliance personnel must periodically assess adherence to internal
procedures and applicable regulatory obligations. Material compliance
findings must be documented and escalated through the appropriate channels.
