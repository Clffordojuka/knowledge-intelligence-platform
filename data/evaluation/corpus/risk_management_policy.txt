RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. Risk Identification
The institution must identify and document risks arising from lending
activities. Risk assessments should consider borrower characteristics,
repayment capacity, credit history, and relevant external factors.

2. Risk Classification
Credit applications must be classified according to the institution's
approved risk categories. Risk classifications must be supported by
documented assessment findings.

3. Exception Escalation
Applications that fall outside established credit policy thresholds must be
escalated to the senior credit approval committee when the applicable
delegated authority requires committee review.

4. Approval Authority
Credit officers may approve applications only within their assigned
delegated approval limits. Applications exceeding those limits require
approval from the appropriate higher authority.

5. Risk Monitoring
The risk management function must periodically review lending portfolio
performance, identify emerging risks, and report significant findings to
management.
