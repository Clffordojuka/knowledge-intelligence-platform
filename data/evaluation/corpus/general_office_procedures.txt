GENERAL OFFICE PROCEDURES

1. Meeting Room Reservations
Employees should reserve meeting rooms through the internal scheduling
system. Reservations should include the meeting title, expected attendance,
date, and duration.

2. Office Equipment
Employees should report faulty office equipment to the facilities team.
Equipment maintenance requests must include the equipment location and
a description of the reported problem.

3. Visitor Management
Visitors must register at the reception desk upon arrival. Hosts are
responsible for notifying reception when their visitors have arrived.

4. Office Supplies
Departments may request stationery and other office supplies through
the standard procurement process. Requests should identify the items
required and the intended department.

5. Workplace Communication
Employees should use approved internal communication channels for routine
administrative announcements and workplace coordination.
